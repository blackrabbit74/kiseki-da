# Preparing Fundraising Materials

## Purpose

Create internally consistent investor materials that connect traction, business assumptions, funding needs, and milestones.

## Deliverable

Return the requested material plus a fact-and-assumption ledger, reconciled financial logic, sources, and unresolved conflicts.

Done when: metrics, ask, spending, milestones, and team facts agree across the requested assets.

Stop and report when: a conflicting key metric prevents a defensible claim; leave that item unresolved while drafting supported sections. Continue independent work and identify the specific blocked action.

## Inputs

Audience and artifact format, current company facts, traction definitions, forecasts, funding ask, use of funds, and milestone plan.

## Decision rules

- Separate actual traction from projections and define the period and denominator of each metric.
- Link spending to achieved milestones rather than allocating an unexplained percentage pie.
- Model downside sensitivity where the case depends on growth, margins, or timing.

## Required procedure

1. Build a compact canonical fact ledger and identify missing assumptions.
2. Draft the requested narrative and calculations for the actual investor audience.
3. Reconcile all numbers and verify current market, legal, or financing claims against authoritative sources with date and jurisdiction.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

Do not invent traction or credentials or imply offering approval. Drafting alone does not authorize investor outreach; preserve any explicit outreach authorization. Preserve the user’s scope and existing authorization.

## Model notes

No model-specific adjustment.
