# Researching Social Discussions

## Purpose

Recover traceable social discussion with context and selection limits.

## Deliverable

Return linked findings, queries, time window, disagreements, and coverage.

Done when: posts are retrieved and sample boundaries are explicit.

Stop and report when: platform access prevents retrieving a post; report available public evidence. Continue independent work and identify the blocked action.

## Inputs

Topic, platforms, dates, language, accounts, and search tools.

## Decision rules

- Split account-focused and keyword-focused searches to preserve disagreement.
- Expand sparse date windows explicitly.
- Read threads when irony, replies, or reposts could alter meaning.

## Required procedure

1. Discover supported platform filters.
2. Retrieve bounded results and deduplicate reposts.
3. Compare themes and corroborate factual claims outside social posts.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

Do not invent posts, engagement counts, or representative sentiment percentages. Preserve the user's scope and existing authorization.

## Model notes

No model-specific adjustment.
