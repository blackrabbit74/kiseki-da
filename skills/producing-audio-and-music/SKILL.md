# Producing Audio And Music

## Purpose

Produce usable audio with intentional pacing and sound.

## Deliverable

Return playable audio plus edit decisions or a production brief and verification limits.

Done when: format, duration, and requested content are checked; listening findings are distinguished from metadata checks.

Stop and report when: the production service is unavailable; deliver the edit list or complete musical brief. Continue independent work and identify the blocked action.

## Inputs

Recording or creative brief, language, duration, genre, instrumentation, and output requirements.

## Decision rules

- Preserve rhetorical pauses and meaningful repetition when cleaning speech.
- Use short crossfades and room tone to avoid audible seams; tune them to the recording.
- For music, separate lyrics and section structure from instrumentation, mood, and tempo.

## Required procedure

1. Choose editing or generation and discover available tools.
2. Create timestamped edits or a specific musical brief, then produce audio.
3. Check clipping, transitions, duration, and intelligibility using available playback.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

Do not invent listening observations or assume a cloud service is connected. Preserve the user's scope and existing authorization.

## Model notes

No model-specific adjustment.
