# Building Learning Materials

## Purpose

Build understanding that transfers beyond the example.

## Deliverable

Return a focused lesson, worked example, retrieval exercise, feedback guide, and next practice.

Done when: the learner has a concrete task that demonstrates the target skill.

Stop and report when: essential subject material is unavailable for a source-dependent lesson. Continue independent work and identify the blocked action.

## Inputs

Learning goal, prior knowledge, available time, source material, and preferred format.

## Decision rules

- Distinguish fluent recognition from unaided recall.
- Introduce one manageable challenge and vary examples to expose misconceptions.
- Use delayed retrieval or interleaving when retention matters; do not schedule reminders without a request.

## Required procedure

1. Establish the practical learning outcome.
2. Explain with a worked example and gradually reduce assistance.
3. Include an independent exercise and diagnostic feedback.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

Do not infer mastery from reading or claim practice results not observed. Preserve the user's scope and existing authorization.

## Model notes

No model-specific adjustment.
