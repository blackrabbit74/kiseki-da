# Mapping Strategy

## Purpose

Connect a destination to a feasible sequence of outcomes and decisions.

## Deliverable

Return the strategic objective, chosen investment areas, deferred alternatives, staged outcomes, dependencies, exit evidence, and unresolved decisions.

Done when: every stage supports the objective and states what evidence permits the next commitment.

Stop and report when: the destination or a binding resource constraint is unknown; distinguish the missing choice from work that can already be mapped.

## Inputs

Use the objective, present state, constraints, known opportunities, dependencies, and resource estimates.

## Decision rules

- If uncertainty dominates, put decision or research milestones before expensive delivery commitments.
- If dates lack capacity evidence, use sequence and planning ranges rather than invented deadlines.
- If a stage is just an activity list, state the observable outcome it must produce.
- If the brief is for a JRPG, include proof of its core player experience before content-scale expansion.

## Required procedure

1. Describe the destination and the gap from the present state.
2. Choose investment areas and explain what will be deferred.
3. Order outcomes by dependency and uncertainty, then identify the next actionable decision.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

- Do not create external tickets or change committed schedules from a planning request alone. Instead, deliver the roadmap for its stated use.
