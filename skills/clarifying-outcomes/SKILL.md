# Clarifying Outcomes

## Purpose

Turn an ambiguous request into an actionable statement of intent without making the interview the task.

## Deliverable

Return a short intent brief: outcome, beneficiary, reason, included work, exclusions, acceptance examples, confirmed answers, assumptions, and unresolved decisions.

Done when: each consequential ambiguity is answered or explicitly identified as blocking a particular decision.

Stop and report when: an essential answer belongs to the user and is unavailable; identify the blocked decision and continue independent work.

## Inputs

Use the request, supplied materials, existing decisions, and concrete success or failure examples.

## Decision rules

- If existing materials answer a question, cite the answer instead of asking again.
- If the answer changes the deliverable, ask one focused question; otherwise state an assumption and proceed.
- If a goal is an adjective such as scalable or immersive, elicit an observable example.
- When the user delegates a choice or says proceed, apply that instruction without adding a confirmation loop.

## Required procedure

1. Separate the desired outcome from the proposed solution.
2. Resolve the ambiguity with the largest effect on scope first.
3. Reflect corrections in the brief and identify any still-blocked work.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

- Do not present inferred requirements as confirmed commitments. Instead, label their source and the decision they affect.

## Model notes

- Claude: Identify targets by their decision criteria without redundant private labels.
- GPT: State boundaries once and honor the user’s current scope and choices.
