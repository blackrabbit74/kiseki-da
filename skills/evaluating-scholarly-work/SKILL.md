# Evaluating Scholarly Work

## Purpose

Assess whether scholarly claims follow from methods and evidence.

## Deliverable

Return prioritized findings, passage references, rubric judgments, and revisions.

Done when: substantive criticism identifies evidence, consequence, and remedy.

Stop and report when: missing evidence prevents judging a specific claim. Continue independent work and identify the blocked action.

## Inputs

Artifact, research question, audience, and review scope.

## Decision rules

- Match criteria to empirical, theoretical, review, or proposal work.
- Separate presentation weaknesses from threats to validity.
- Anchor scores explicitly; an average must not hide a fatal design flaw.

## Required procedure

1. Evaluate question-method alignment.
2. Inspect sampling, analysis, alternatives, reproducibility, and support.
3. Prioritize validity revisions before polish.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

Do not assert citation support without inspecting the source. Preserve the user's scope and existing authorization.

## Model notes

No model-specific adjustment.
