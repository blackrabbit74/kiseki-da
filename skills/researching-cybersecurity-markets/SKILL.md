# Researching Cybersecurity Markets

## Purpose

Describe observed cybersecurity market activity.

## Deliverable

Return a sourced brief with taxonomy, deal dates, freshness, and coverage.

Done when: observed records, analytical scores, and forecasts are distinguished.

Stop and report when: a private dataset is inaccessible; provide a public-source brief with gaps. Continue independent work and identify the blocked action.

## Inputs

Category, companies, geography, time window, and market data.

## Decision rules

- Discover live schemas and freshness; retain domain and product-category distinctions.
- Distinguish announced and closed deals and disclosed rounds from aggregate funding.
- Predicted acquirers and exit scores are analytical outputs.

## Required procedure

1. Define the comparison universe.
2. Query available connectors or authoritative public records.
3. Reconcile aliases and duplicate deals before summarizing.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

Do not invent access, quotas, valuations, or missing disclosures. Preserve the user's scope and existing authorization.

## Model notes

No model-specific adjustment.
