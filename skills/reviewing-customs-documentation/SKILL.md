# Reviewing Customs Documentation

## Purpose

Prepare or review trade documents and customs reasoning for a specified shipment, jurisdiction, and date.

## Deliverable

Return draft documents or a discrepancy register, classification rationale, source citations, origin and value assumptions, and unresolved filing requirements.

Done when: shipment identities, quantities, values, origin, and responsibilities reconcile and legal claims cite current applicable authority.

Stop and report when: a material product fact, screening match, or jurisdictional requirement remains unresolved; hold the affected classification or filing. Continue independent work and identify the specific blocked action.

## Inputs

Product specifications and composition, origin evidence, route, parties, values, invoices, packing details, terms, and shipment date.

## Decision rules

- Classify from product characteristics and applicable heading notes before seeking a favorable duty rate.
- Preferential origin is a separate evidence question from shipping origin.
- Named trade terms allocate responsibilities but do not replace the governing law or all contract provisions.

## Required procedure

1. Reconcile invoice, packing, transport, and origin records.
2. Verify current tariff, classification rules, restrictions, and filing requirements with official jurisdiction-specific sources.
3. Document alternatives and missing evidence; prepare consistent drafts and validate any authorized submission response.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

Do not fabricate origin or lower values, declare an unresolved screening hit cleared, or claim professional certification. Preserve the user’s scope and existing authorization.

## Model notes

No model-specific adjustment.
