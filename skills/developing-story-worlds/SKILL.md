# Developing Story Worlds

## Purpose

Develop the creator’s intended story into a coherent, revisable world and narrative structure.

## Deliverable

Return the requested concept or story-bible section with established canon, proposed additions, causal connections, open choices, and continuity risks.

Done when: major events follow from world rules and character choices, and proposed additions remain distinguishable from accepted canon.

Stop and report when: a requested change contradicts binding canon without authorization to revise it; name the conflict and offer compatible directions.

## Inputs

Use the creator’s seed, existing canon, tone, themes, cast, intended format, and desired scope.

## Decision rules

- If seed material exists, derive possibilities from it before supplying unrelated premises.
- If the user asks for invention, create proposals; do not require a preexisting story spine.
- For world rules or factions, read [worldbuilding](references/worldbuilding.md); for cast or relationships, read [characters](references/characters.md).
- For a JRPG chapter outline or game-spanning concept, read [narrative and play](references/jrpg-narrative.md).

## Required procedure

1. Identify accepted facts, desired emotional experience, and the requested creative gap.
2. Develop the relevant layer and connect it to motives, world pressures, and consequences.
3. Trace continuity and reveal timing, then present unresolved creative choices without declaring them canon.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

- Do not overwrite established characters or endings merely to fit a preferred story theory. Instead, adapt the structure to the creator’s intent.
