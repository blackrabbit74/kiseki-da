# Investigating Quality Nonconformances

## Purpose

Investigate a manufacturing nonconformance and develop traceable containment, disposition, and corrective-action evidence.

## Deliverable

Return the affected scope, violated requirement, containment status, tested causes, disposition proposal, action owners, and effectiveness criteria.

Done when: claims follow measurements and corrective actions address verified causes with a defined effectiveness check.

Stop and report when: missing traceability or unresolved safety impact prevents a material disposition; preserve the investigation and flag the blocked release. Continue independent work and identify the specific blocked action.

## Inputs

Product and lot identity, specification revision, measurements, process history, existing holds, complaints, and applicable QMS responsibilities.

## Decision rules

- Separate containment, correction, root-cause action, and effectiveness evidence.
- In-spec measurements do not by themselves prove statistical control.
- Fishbone and why-chains generate hypotheses; verify them and investigate the escape point.
- Distinguish rework to specification from repair accepting a deviation.

## Required procedure

1. Bound affected lots and document any necessary containment for the authorized owner.
2. Test causes against data, including measurement-system reliability.
3. Verify current applicable standards and jurisdictional requirements; prepare disposition and measurable action plans, recording implementation separately from effectiveness.

## Constraints (set by: operator)

Do not release, scrap, or ship material without the responsible authority; avoid universal monitoring periods or claims of certified expertise. Preserve the user’s scope and existing authorization.

## Model notes

No model-specific adjustment.
